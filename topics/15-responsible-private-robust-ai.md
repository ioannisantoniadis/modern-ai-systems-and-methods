# Responsible, Private, and Robust AI

## Core Idea

Modern AI systems affect people. Responsible AI studies whether models are fair, private, interpretable, robust, secure, and aligned with user expectations.

## Fairness

Common concerns:

- Historical bias.
- Representation bias.
- Measurement bias.
- Allocation harms.
- Quality-of-service gaps across groups.

Common metrics:

- Demographic parity.
- Equalized odds.
- Equal opportunity.
- Calibration by group.

## Privacy

Techniques and principles:

- Data minimization.
- Anonymization and aggregation.
- Differential privacy.
- Federated learning.
- On-device inference.
- Secure aggregation.

## Interpretability

Methods:

- Coefficients and feature importance.
- SHAP and LIME.
- Counterfactual explanations.
- Saliency maps.
- Probing and mechanistic interpretability for deep models.

## Robustness And Security

Topics:

- Distribution shift.
- Adversarial examples.
- Prompt injection.
- Data poisoning.
- Model extraction.
- Jailbreaks.
- Abuse monitoring.

## Safety And Alignment

For GenAI:

- Refusal behavior.
- Factuality.
- Toxicity and policy compliance.
- Reward hacking.
- Human preference validation.
- Red teaming.

## Interview Check

Be able to explain that fairness, privacy, and robustness are not one metric each. They require defining the affected users, failure modes, constraints, measurement strategy, and mitigation plan.

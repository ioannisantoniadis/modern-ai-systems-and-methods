# Interview Checkpoints

Use these prompts to test whether each area is part of your working knowledge.

## Probabilistic Modeling

- Explain likelihood, prior, posterior, and evidence.
- Compare generative and discriminative models.
- When would Naive Bayes outperform a more flexible model?
- How do you model missing or noisy labels?

## Graphical Models And Latent Variables

- What is a latent variable?
- How does EM work at a high level?
- Compare mixture models, HMMs, and topic models.
- Why are conditional independence assumptions useful?

## Sampling And Inference

- Why is exact inference often impossible?
- Explain Monte Carlo estimation.
- Explain Metropolis-Hastings at a high level.
- Compare MCMC and variational inference.

## Representation Learning

- Compare PCA, matrix factorization, and autoencoders.
- What is a VAE?
- What is contrastive learning?
- How do embeddings become production features?

## Sequences And State

- What is the Markov assumption?
- Compare Markov chains, HMMs, RNNs, and transformers.
- How would you detect time-series anomalies?
- Why should validation splits be temporal?

## Reinforcement Learning

- Define state, action, reward, policy, and value.
- Compare contextual bandits and RL.
- Explain Q-learning.
- Why is offline RL hard?
- How does RLHF relate to preference learning?

## Causality

- Why is prediction not causation?
- What is confounding?
- What is a propensity score?
- How do A/B tests connect to causal inference?

## ML Systems

- What is train/serve skew?
- What do you monitor after launch?
- How do you debug a model regression?
- What is the difference between model evaluation and product evaluation?
